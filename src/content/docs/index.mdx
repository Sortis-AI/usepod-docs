Frontier models are temporary monopolies; open-weight models are commodities, and
commodities get cheaper when markets are allowed to work. UsePod is a two-sided
marketplace that lets supply meet demand directly and drives the price of inference
toward the cost of production, while a drop-in API keeps your existing agents and
tools running unchanged. [Why inference is becoming a commodity →](/concepts/inference-commodity/)
